# tailshare — Feature ideas

The loose backlog the roadmap draws from. **Ideas, not commitments** — nothing here is ordered or decided. When one is picked up it becomes a lettered step in `roadmap.md` and is removed from here.

- **More platforms.** Windows and Mac come first ("for now"); other devices on the tailnet could follow later. iPhone and iPad are step E, through Shortcuts.
- **A tailshare CLI for headless machines.** A server on the tailnet (like the always-on home Linux server) could send and receive with tailshare from a terminal, over the same Core the Windows app uses (Finn, 2026-11-18).
- **Trusted and blocked devices in the UI.** Step A keeps both lists in a settings file that can be edited by hand, with no interface. A screen to see them and un-trust or block a device would sit on top of that. For now, un-trusting means editing the file or reinstalling the app.
- **Recently received items in the popup.** For now the notification is the only record of a received item; a short list of recent receives (text to re-copy, files to open) could live in the tray / menu-bar popup.
